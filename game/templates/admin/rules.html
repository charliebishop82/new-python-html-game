Attack Resolution

  1. Choose effective STR for melee or effective AGI for ranged.
  2. Roll d20 + modifier + D&D-style proficiency (+2, then +1 every four levels) against Armor Class. Outfit tier adds +0 AC at Levels 1–3, +1 at 4–9, +2 at 10–15, and +3 at 16–18.
  3. On a hit, roll weapon damage, add its level-tier bonus (L1–3 +0 through L16–18 +5), and add every active typed bonus-damage component.
  4. Apply critical multipliers, each componentâ€™s resistance or weakness, enemy scaling, HP, durability, and defeat checks.
  5. An Extra Attack effect repeats the full accuracy and damage process once.

World-Boss Attempt Rewards

Only actual post-resistance damage accepted by the shared HP pool counts. Completed-round rewards are capped at {{ settings.get('WORLD_BOSS_ROUNDS_MAX',10) }} rounds. Equipment and perk XP/credit multipliers apply after the performance calculation.

XP = {{ settings.get('WORLD_BOSS_ATTEMPT_XP',10) }} + damage × {{ settings.get('WORLD_BOSS_XP_PER_DAMAGE',1.0) }} + rounds × {{ settings.get('WORLD_BOSS_XP_PER_ROUND',2.0) }}. Credits = {{ settings.get('WORLD_BOSS_ATTEMPT_CREDITS',5) }} + damage × {{ settings.get('WORLD_BOSS_CREDITS_PER_DAMAGE',0.25) }} + rounds × {{ settings.get('WORLD_BOSS_CREDITS_PER_ROUND',1.0) }}.

Opposed Actions

Steal and Escape compare d20 + floor(AGI ÷ 2) + floor(LCK ÷ 2). Observe also adds floor(PER ÷ 2) to both sides. The actor must win strictly; ties fail.

Progression

Base XP is multiplied by level difference: +10% for each opponent level above; −15% for each level below, with a 10% minimum. Combined equipped-special and perk XP multipliers apply afterward, including eligible steal, defeat, and world-boss attempt XP.

Every level grants one permanent core-stat choice. Levels divisible by three also grant one unowned perk whose content rank is no higher than the characterâ€™s new level plus two. Imported perk magnitudes pass through the global perk scale and safety caps before use.
