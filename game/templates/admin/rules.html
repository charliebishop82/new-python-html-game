Attack Resolution

  1. Choose STR for melee or AGI for ranged.
  2. Roll d20 + modifier + D&D-style proficiency (+2, then +1 every four levels) against Armor Class.
  3. If applicable, resolve the opposed dodge check.
  4. Roll damage die + modifier; criticals double weapon damage.
  5. Apply resistance, weakness, enemy damage scaling, HP, durability, and defeat checks.

Opposed Actions

Steal and Escape compare d20 + floor(AGI ÷ 2) + floor(LCK ÷ 2). Observe also adds floor(PER ÷ 2) to both sides. The actor must win strictly; ties fail.

Progression

Base XP is multiplied by level difference: +10% for each opponent level above; −15% for each level below, with a 10% minimum. Equipped XP multipliers apply afterward.
