Turns and Action Points

AP pays for major activities. The normal daily allowance is restored at midnight UTC, scheduled trickles add AP during the day, and AP never exceeds its configured cap.

Maximum AP = base daily AP + floor(END ÷ 2), limited by the AP cap.

Entering Boss or PvP combat pays the listed AP cost. The Shop and Auction House charge when you enter; transactions during that visit are free. Combat actions normally use turns rather than additional AP, except actions that explicitly display an AP cost.

Combat Flow

  1. You enter a Boss, Minion, or PvP encounter and pay its admission AP. A roaming minion may interrupt Boss, PvP, Weekly World Boss, or Shop activity; the original activity resumes after victory, escape, or stalemate and is cancelled by defeat. The interruption adds no separate admission cost.
  2. Each round rolls initiative for both sides.
  3. The faster side acts, defeat is checked, and the other side acts if combat is still active.
  4. Round effects and durability are applied, then the next round begins.
  5. Combat ends at 1 HP, on a successful escape, or through an applicable round-limit resolution.
Initiative = d20 + floor(AGI ÷ 2) + equipment/status initiative bonuses.

Attack and damage

Melee attack = d20 + floor(STR ÷ 2) + proficiency. Ranged attack = d20 + floor(AGI ÷ 2) + proficiency. Proficiency starts at +2 and rises by +1 every four levels. The attack hits when its total equals or exceeds Armor Class.

A hit rolls the weapon’s damage die and adds the same relevant stat modifier. Critical hits double normal weapon damage. Resistance halves matching damage from one resistance source; stacked resistance reduces it to the configured floor. A matching boss weakness doubles damage.

Some eligible attacks also receive an opposed dodge check. The defender must roll strictly higher; a tie favors the attacker.

PvP round-limit resolution

If PvP reaches its round limit, it may be extended for AP or resolved by score.

Score = remaining HP percentage × {{ settings.get('COMBAT_WIN_HP_WEIGHT', 0.40) }} + share of total damage dealt × {{ settings.get('COMBAT_WIN_DMG_WEIGHT', 0.60) }}.

Experience, Rewards, and Leveling

Victory XP begins with the opponent’s level and encounter type, then adjusts for level difference and equipped XP bonuses. Higher-level opponents add 10% per level; lower-level opponents reduce rewards by 15% per level, with a 10% minimum.

Base victory XP: Minion {{ settings.get('MINION_XP_PER_LEVEL', 20) }} × level; Boss {{ settings.get('BOSS_XP_PER_LEVEL', 35) }} × level; PvP {{ settings.get('PVP_XP_PER_LEVEL', 25) }} × opponent level.

Successful steals and completed defeats can award smaller XP amounts. XP never decreases. Crossing a threshold raises your level and requires one permanent stat choice. At Levels 3, 6, 9, and every third level afterward, you also choose one unowned perk whose listed level is no higher than your new level plus two. Perk effects are permanent and are included anywhere the matching equipment-style bonus is calculated.

Weekly World Boss

All participating characters attack one shared HP pool. An attempt costs {{ settings.get('AP_COST_WORLD_BOSS', 4) }} AP, uses normal boss combat rules, and grants a small attempt reward without ordinary drops. Damage shown on the leaderboard is actual post-resistance damage, capped at the boss's remaining HP.

The event closes when the boss falls or at the weekly Monday UTC turnover. First, second, and third place are ranked by total damage; audited deterministic coin flips break exact ties. First place chooses one of the boss's three unique artifacts, second chooses from the remaining two, and third receives the last. Each winner also gains a full independent level. A missed 12-hour choice defaults to the highest-credit remaining prize.

Steal cannot remove weekly prize artifacts during combat. If another player lands the final blow while your attempt is open, your fight closes immediately and the shared event log identifies the finisher.

Equipment and Durability

Weapons define attack type, damage die, and damage category. Armor supplies AC and resistances. Specials can alter statistics, initiative, criticals, rewards, durability, and other rules. Broken or worn equipment becomes less useful and should be repaired at the Blacksmith.

New characters receive and automatically equip a starter weapon and armor. Equipment can be changed normally outside combat or by spending a combat turn.

Shop, Tavern, Blacksmith, and Events

  • Shop: pay AP once to enter, then buy and sell freely during the visit.
  • Auction House: pay AP to enter. You may hold up to two unequipped special items in public 24- or 48-hour auctions. Bids reserve credits; outbid credits are returned automatically. Auction-held items cannot be equipped, sold, dropped, stolen, or lose durability.
  • Tavern: spend AP and credits to restore health.
  • Blacksmith: spend AP and credits to repair durability.
  • Random events: may change AP, credits, stats, gear, or temporary effects; the result text states the applied effect and duration. Equipment labeled “Random event chance” modifies these events, not roaming-minion interruptions.
  • Special items: are globally unique; only one live copy may exist at a time.

Personal and World Logs

YOU entries affect your character. AGAINST YOU entries were initiated by somebody else. WORLD entries are public announcements. SYSTEM entries explain rules and services.

The dashboard retains the current day’s events and combat rounds. Midnight archives the day for administrator review and starts a fresh player display. Open the World Log from the bottom bar to browse public announcements.
